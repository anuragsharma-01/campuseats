# CampusEats Brief

## What

CampusEats is a campus food ordering system. It helps students find available food, place orders, and track their orders. It also helps campus food providers manage menus and incoming orders.

## Who

### Students
Students are the main users. They can:
- View available food and menus.
- Search and select food items.
- Add items to an order.
- Place orders.
- Track order status.
- View previous orders.

### Food Providers
Food providers can:
- Create and manage menu items.
- View incoming orders.
- Accept or reject orders.
- Update order status.
- Manage food availability.

### Administrator
The administrator manages the overall system. They can:
- Manage users.
- Manage food providers.
- Monitor the system.
- Maintain system information.

## Main Flow

A student logs into CampusEats and views the available food options. The student selects food items and adds them to an order. The student places the order and completes payment if required.

The food provider receives the order, accepts it, prepares the food, and updates the order status. The student can then track the order and collect the food from the selected location.

## Purpose

CampusEats brings food discovery, ordering, and order tracking into one system. It provides students with a convenient way to order food and gives food providers a structured way to manage menus and orders.
